In 2020, I joined Trojan Horse Was Unicorn (THU), taking on the challenge of transforming their physical community into a digital experience.

THU began as a small gathering of artists 15 years ago and has evolved into a globally recognized community in the digital entertainment industry.

The challenge

Create an engaging online platform that would serve as the foundation for growing and nurturing a community centered around an annual in-person event.

Through extensive workshops and brainstorming sessions, we identified these key objectives:

  • Capture THU's vibrant community spirit in our digital presence
  • Expand into online-only events, especially in response to COVID-19
  • Develop a central community hub to attract and retain members
  • Design a bold, creative, and unique user experience that embodies THU's values

The 2022 main event app

We developed a companion mobile app that complements the main event experience. Built with React Native for both iOS and Android platforms, the app maintains the event's distinctive aesthetic while providing essential mobile functionality.

Conclusion

The project was a remarkable success, transforming THU's physical community into a thriving digital ecosystem. We successfully created an engaging platform that maintained the unique spirit of THU while expanding its reach globally.

Working with this extraordinary community was both challenging and rewarding. The experience provided valuable insights into building digital platforms that foster genuine connections and community engagement.

The initial concept has since evolved, and you can explore the latest iteration of our work at the THU website, where the core principles of our original design continue to shape the community's digital experience.
